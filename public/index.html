Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

What this site uses

The site displays public Roblox community data, including group information, public roles, public experience information, visits, player counts, and Roblox-hosted thumbnails. Looking up a username requests that public account's visible ReWorked-Games role from Roblox.

Optional Roblox connection

If the community owner enables Roblox OAuth and you choose to connect, Roblox performs the sign-in. This site does not receive or request your Roblox password. It keeps only the returned basic account identity needed for the session, such as user ID, username, display name, and profile link.

Cookies and retention

When connected, a secure, HTTP-only session cookie keeps you signed in for up to one hour. Session information is held in memory and is removed when it expires, when you log out, or when the service restarts. Roblox access and refresh tokens are not stored.

Service providers

Roblox supplies the public data, account connection, and image assets. Render hosts this website. Their services have their own privacy policies and terms.

Choices

You can avoid the optional account connection, use the public pages without signing in, or log out from the Account page to remove the active site session.

Changes and contact

We may revise this policy when the site changes. For privacy questions about this community hub, contact the community owner through the official Roblox community page.
